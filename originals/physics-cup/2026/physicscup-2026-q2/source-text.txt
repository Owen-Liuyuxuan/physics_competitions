By Jaan Kalda
Consider a gas with initial number density
n
and temperature
T
. At the initial moment, the gas is spatially uniform, while the macroscopic velocity is parallel to the
x
-axis with a profile given by
v_x(x)=v_0 \sin(kx)
. The molecules can be treated as rigid spheres of mass
m
and radius
r
.
What condition for
v_0
must be satisfied so that, at a certain moment and location, a multi-stream flow arises? A flow is considered “multi-stream” at a point
x
if the local velocity distribution function
f(v_x)
has at least two local maxima, where each maximum is at least twice the value of the local minimum between them.
Assume that
v_0\gg \sqrt {k_BT/m}
. It is sufficient to estimate the answer to an order of magnitude; exact numerical prefactors are not required.
