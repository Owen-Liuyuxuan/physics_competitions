Problem 1 –  Interaction force
In total, 233 solutions were submitted for problem 1, of which 63 were correct. Congratulations to everyone who managed to solve it!
There were 41 correct solutions submitted before the first hint was published, and only those were considered as candidates for the best solution awards. Additionally, since there were so many well-written solutions to choose from, hand-written solutions were not considered this time, sorry about that!
A vast majority of solutions were based on Legendre polynomials expansion of the Laplace equation solution in spherical coordinates. That was definitely not the intended solution, but nevertheless a valid brute-force approach. If you can solve by brute-force, why waste time trying to figure out something trickier! Still, since Physics Cup is by intention were you show off your creativity, such brute force solutions did not warrant a best solution award per se; still, below are also linked some examples of well-written solutions using Legendre polynomials.
While there were no intended solutions (the one
written by me – JK – can be found here
), there were solutions without Legendre polynomials. These were using the fact that the solution to the boundary value problem (electrostatic potential on the ball’s surface takes the values dictated by the electric field inside the sphere) outside the sphere is unique, and by guessing – and verifying – that this condition is satisfied by the field provided by an ideal quadrupole at the centre of the ball. These were the solutions provided by
Ethan Lu
,
Hui Xiang Sim
, and
Siddhaarth Dharani
.

The total bonus of 1 pts is evenly distributed between these eight contestants.
