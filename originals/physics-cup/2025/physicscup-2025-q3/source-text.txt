A monoatomic gas fills a cylinder of height
H
under a piston. The piston oscillates periodically up and down with amplitude
a
: during the first half-period, it moves upward with a constant speed
u
, and during the second half-period, it moves downward with the same constant speed
u
. Initially, the root-mean-square (RMS) speed of the molecules is
v
. How much time
t
will it take for the RMS speed to double? Use the following model assumptions: the walls and the piston are perfect heat insulators and have zero heat capacity; the surface of the piston is perfectly flat; the mean free path of the molecules
\lambda
satisfies the following conditions:
H\gg\lambda\gg a
and
\lambda\gg \frac{H^2}{vt}
; additionally,
v\gg u
.
