A satellite orbits a planet. At point
A
, its speed is
v_1
. At point
B
, its speed is
v_2
and its velocity vector forms a right angle with the velocity vector at point
A
. At point
C
, the velocity is exactly opposite to the velocity at point
A
, with a magnitude of
v_3
. Find the eccentricity of the orbit. Also determine the exact numerical value of the eccentricity when
v_1 = 1
km/s,
v_2 = 2
km/s, and
v_3 = 3
km/s.
