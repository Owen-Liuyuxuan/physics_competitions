Problem 1 – Diving cylinder
In total, 136 solutions were submitted for problem 1, out of which 22 were correct. This problem turned out to be one of the most difficult ones of all time, so
congratulations to everyone who still managed to solve this problem!
The first two hints were very subtle while the third hint mentioned the most important keyword, added mass. Thus,
the Best Solution Awards
were selected from the two submissions sent before the third hint was published.  These were the solutions of
Val Karan
and
Guy Ginzberg
. [Although this was not considered as a demerit when deciding bonuses, G.G’s solution does not follow here and there typographical standards, such as: all symbols denoting physical quantities should be in italic while operator names (such as “sin”), the differential symbols “d”, mathematical constants such as e=2.718… and imaginary unit “i” should be upright; also, LaTeX gives much more professional look than Word.] Both of them later updated their solutions with alternative approaches; what are linked here are their final submissions. Both of them have written the theoretical analysis really well, but in both cases, the data analysis part is a little lacking, with Val Karan being slightly better in this department. That’s why Val Karan gets 0.5 of the bonus, and Guy Ginzberg – 0.3 The remaining 0.2 goes to the solution with the best data analysis, submitted by
Michał
Kołodziejczyk
. (Since the discussion of data analysis in the hints was minimal, no time limit was used when choosing the solution with the best data analysis.)  Additionally, solutions of
Karlo Maksimović
and
Nick Hartmann
show a good theoretical and data analysis parts and are therefore also published, earning both of them 0.1 bonus.
