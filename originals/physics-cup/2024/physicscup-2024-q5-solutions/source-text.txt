Problem 5 – Water pipe
In total, 27 solutions were submitted for problem 5, of which 10 were correct. Congratulations to everyone who managed to solve it!
There were no correct solutions before the first hint was published, only one solution before the second hint was published, and three more solutions before the fourth hint was published. The best solutions were selected out of those four submissions. Three of them were explained in detail and reader-friendly (also typeset in LaTeX) – well deserving bonus points. The bonus breakdown was determined based on the time of submission (one hint is noticeably better than three hints!) and on how well the solution was explained: the bonus of 0.6 goes to
Lukas Schicht
, 0.25 – to
Luka Passek-Kumerički
, and 0.15 – to
Konstantin Rodionenko
.
