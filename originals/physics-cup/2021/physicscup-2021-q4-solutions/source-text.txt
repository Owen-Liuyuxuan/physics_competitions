Problem 4 – Shock wave in an electron gas
The best solution award for problem 4. is shared between
Nikita Semenin
– a nicely written full and compact solution covering both parts A and B;
Huiyuan Wang
– a nice phase diagram and compact explanation of how the adiabatic invariant works (the solution of part 4A is found on pg 1-2);
Zhening Li
– conservation of the adiabatic invariant derived from basic mechanics principles for the given process;
Lotus Chen
– a simple solution based on the quantum-mechanical version of the adiabatic invariant;
Oliwier Urbanski
–
a special bonus for his
simulation applet
