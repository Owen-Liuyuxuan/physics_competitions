Problem 4 – Spaceship
In total, 67 solutions were submitted for problem 3, out of which 31 were correct.
Congratulations to everyone who managed to solve this problem!
The Best Solution Awards
were selected from the 14 submissions sent before the first hint was published. Since there were so many good solutions, at the first selection stage I excluded those submissions which lacked documentation details (this time, all hand-written solutions fell into this category). The next elimination criterion was if the
analytically exact
answer was obtained (out of the papers lacking the exact answer, two are still published due to additional reasons, see below). The remaining 7 solutions  – each of which earns 1/7th part of the bonus) can be divided into the following categories.
First, solutions based on the relativistic addition of 3-velocities:
Eppu Leinonen
(with nice additions like calculation of the travel time),
Tejas Acharya
(using rapidities instead of velocities, the solution has also a nice discussion of Wigner rotations),
Alptürk Eski
.
The solutions based on the Lorentz transform of 4-velocities:
Luka Passek-Kumerički
and
Lukas Schicht
(who also calculates the proper travel time).
The solutions based on the Lorentz transform of (t,x) 4-vectors:
Vlad-Stefan Oros
and
Tomas Babelis
.
Additionally, the solutions of
Anindya Guria
and
Gil Ronen
are so well written that it would be a shame not to publish them (the submission of GR comes with two solutions, one using relativistic addition of velocities, and the other one – Lorentz transform of t-x 4-vector).
During the period before the first hint was published, there were no solutions based on Lorentz invariants. The first such solution was submitted by
Filip Baciak
– which is also published. As usual, all those solutions which are published but did not receive the best-solution-bonus will earn the bonus factor of exp(0.1).
Finally, here is
my (JK) own hand-written solution
– the intended solution based on Lorentz invariants.
