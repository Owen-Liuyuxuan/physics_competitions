Problem 1 – Sinking cylinder
In total, 290 solutions were submitted for problem 1, out of which 70  were correct.
Congratulations to everyone who managed to solve this problem!
The Best Solution Awards
were selected from the 18 submissions sent before any of the hints were published. This time, the Best Solution Award was divided into four equal parts of one quarter each [corresponding to the bonus factor exp(1/4)]. The winners are as follows.
Luka Passek-Kumerički
– very thoroughly written analysis including estimates of the added mass and discussion of why the velocity field is potential, i.e. has zero vorticity.
Eyal Walach
– shows that the square root of the distance from the bottom performs half a period of an harmonic oscillation.
Chung Wang Lam
and
礼中 付
– improved accuracy of the result by no longer neglecting the inertia of the cylinder.
Apart from these, there were many more solutions worth publishing, so here is a selection [everyone listed below gets a publication bonus of exp(0.1)].
Vlad-Stefan Oros
– well written, good analysis of the process, including the analysis of the added mass.
Guglielmo Di Grazia
– well written, includes a discussion of the vorticity of the velocity field, and of the added mass.
Luca Milanese
– a minimalist solutions for those who prefer short texts.
Liao Haoding
– a solution based on the Euler equation and calculation of the pressure distribution. Although a handwritten text, a useful read if you want to know how to solve this problem correctly using Newton’s second law and the pressure force.
