Problem 5 – Light sources
In total, 35 solutions were submitted for problem 5, out of which 32 were correct.
Congratulations to everyone who managed to solve this problem!
The Best Solution Awards
were selected from the 19 submissions sent before the first hint was published. Since the problem was fairly straightforward, not much variety in approaches was possible. Indeed, all the solutions were following the same approach. Therefore, the best solutions were chosen based on additional insight given in the solutions. There were three solutions providing insight exceeding what was strictly needed for the solution, those of
Lukas Schicht
,
Marinus Lehmann
and
Lincoln Liu
. Marinus discussed if the assumption of neglecting the intensity dependence on distance from the source was reasonable while Lukas and Lincoln discussed the question if it is possible to have such local intensity maxima which are not global maxima, i.e. the waves from the three sources are not in the same phase. Although Lincoln has some typos in his analysis of the local maxima, the general framework of his approach remains valid. Since Lukas has a very nice proof, he will get 0.4 of the bonus while Marinus and Lincoln share the remaining 0.6 between them equally. Additionally, there were three very well written solutions with step-by-step instructions how to construct the positions of the sources, provided by
Anindya Guria
and
Eppu Leinonen
(each of them receives the 0.1-bonus).
