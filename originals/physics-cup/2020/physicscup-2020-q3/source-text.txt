A small ball of mass
m
is suspended on an in-extensible massless thread of length
l
. The thread is very thin, flexible, and has a breaking force limit of
T_b = 9mg
, where
g
denotes the free fall acceleration. In the initial moment the thread is horizontal as shown in the figure, and the ball velocity
v = v_0
is directed downward. It is known that the thread breaks in one of the four positions shown in the figure with dashed lines, when the angle between the thread and the vertical is  60
^\circ
; friction is to be neglected. Find all the possible values for
v_0
. (A solution is considered to be correct only if
all the suitable values
are
algebraically expressed
, and no extra/invalid values are included.)
