A spherical ball of radius
R=1
cm is made from a NdFeB alloy. The figure below shows the left upper quadrant (
H<0
,
B>0
) of the hysteresis loop of this alloy for two temperatures,
T_1= 20^\circ
C,  and
T_2= 120^\circ
C . Initially, the ball has been magnetized by putting it into a very strong homogeneous magnetic field (with
B\approx 3
T). Then, the external magnetic field is removed while the temperature of the ball is kept equal to
T_1= 20^\circ
C . After that, the ball is heated up to the temperature
T_2= 120^\circ
C . By how much did the magnetic dipole moment of the ball change due to heating?
